# Product Roadmap

The detailed 90-day plan, milestones, pilot metrics and scope guardrails are maintained in [THREE_MONTH_ROADMAP.md](./THREE_MONTH_ROADMAP.md).

## Product priorities

1. **Trust:** payment reconciliation, reliable job state, transparent escrow and support.
2. **Liquidity:** ensure enough relevant workers for a narrow geography/category before expanding coverage.
3. **Conversion:** reduce Poster job-posting/payment friction and Worker discovery/application friction.
4. **Retention:** reliable completed shifts, two-sided reviews and repeat posting.
5. **Operational leverage:** admin reconciliation, support workflows and notification reliability.

## Explicit non-goals for the first pilot

- Do not build a complex AI matching platform before there is sufficient marketplace data.
- Do not launch withdrawals before a payout provider, accounting controls and reconciliation are approved.
- Do not claim KYC or anti-fraud capabilities without a real provider and independent verification.
- Do not expand to multiple regions/categories before one local cohort achieves reliable fulfillment.
